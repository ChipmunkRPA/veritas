eight sequential neural extraction batches and eight separate data-only replay
batches all completed successfully. Every terminal profile has exit0, no guard

not a new manipulation-detection accuracy result.

Each picture keeps its native index, PTS, recorded face/mouth coordinates,
crop/input/feature commitments and actual array row privately. Each supported
speech position keeps its exact source audio origin and ten retained tick
routes. No true mask or edit interval chose which pictures were forwarded.
The five short-source edit conditions remain unavailable, without substituted
inputs or originality conclusions.

Extraction took249.905seconds summed across the eight guarded workers.
The largest sampled descendant RSS was948,142,080bytes; the lowest sampled
available RAM was6,699,909,120bytes. These are observed samples, not guaranteed
peaks. The900-second/6-GiB neural limits and all initial/running reserves were
unchanged. Qualification separately used10.719seconds and ten neural inputs.

The separate replay checked every native RGB, crop reconstruction, normalized
image input, feature-array row, shared-pixel cache assignment and eligible
cached speech summary/time route. It reused recorded landmarks and vectors;
it did not independently rerun the face or image/speech networks. It is a
second local program, not an outside human evaluation. No outside reviewer
has participated in this phase.

The next comparison must fit separate visual-only, concatenation and explicit
interaction classifiers from fitting sources, calibrate them separately, and
measure false alarms, missed edits, localization and unassessed scope on the
locked groups. Current checks remain the baseline. Centred edits from one
generator are insufficient for a replacement claim: fresh placement and
generator transfer tests and the full application flow are still required.
Cached speech summaries may depend on the whole input, so timestamps identify
indexed evidence support rather than proving a strictly local phonetic cause.

All source media, individual media fingerprints, coordinates and face/voice
feature vectors remain private on Seagate. Public files contain code,
aggregates and whole-artifact commitments only. Customer confidence and model
defaults are unchanged; the protected audio rollback hash still matches.
Publication is by manual push, with Actions disabled and no external outreach.
