# Mouth-region sequence comparison: frozen development decision

This is a paired development screen on all 48 planned cases from eight consumed
CREMA-D actors, prepared under `mouth-edit-v4-development.md`, generation 4.1.
Its two AI conditions include synchronized redraws and retimed mouths. Ordinary
local light/resizing and a constant audio delay are non-AI controls. Do not
relabel an aligned generated mouth as genuine or a mux delay as AI generation.

Reuse the actual production face-tracking and face-sequence adapters in an
isolated research context, with their verified MIT YuNet/Apache-2.0 DINOv2 Small
weights. Use the same uniform source frame centres, native triplets, aligned
112-pixel face crops and 8x8 normalized feature tokens for both statistics.
A scoped capture calls the original window function unchanged and reads its
input arguments afterward. The generalized statistic with the original mask
must exactly reproduce its baseline result. This is adapter/statistic evidence,
not a claim that the full prepaid customer flow has passed for a new method.

Only the feature region changes: the original central mask has rows/columns
2:6; the candidate uses rows 5:8 and columns 2:6, covering the aligned mouth and
lower face. Both subtract 0.60 times the surrounding-token change and 0.35 times
the region's before/after change. All temporal spans, contexts, consecutive
input conditions, sorting and maximum retained windows remain identical.
The existing baseline point is frozen. Choose the candidate review point just
above the largest non-AI development case maximum, floored at zero; do not use
AI responses to optimize it. That internal feature-distance point is not an
authenticity percentage or public screening claim.

Compare first flagged intervals with the known AI edit range. Temporal IoU at
least 0.2 counts as localized. Unassessed inputs and positives without flags
remain misses. For spatial pointing, take the chronological middle of the
selected window's during centres, project the measured token rectangle through
the recorded face alignment into native pixels and compare with the exact
changed-pixel mask at that frame. Missing flags, missing masks or a representative
outside the edit receive zero overlap. The rectangles describe measured feature
positions; self-attention features are not pixel-level causal attribution.

Advance only with at least three additional localized AI cases, at most one
lost baseline localization, no additional unassessed cases, and at most one
additional non-AI flag in each control condition. Additionally require at least
0.01 improvement in mean spatial IoU over all planned AI cases, a defined point
and no execution failures. Report synchronized and retimed AI outcomes separately.
Preserve every planned status and the source-connected denominators. Eight
actors and one editing family cannot establish broad generalization.

Run the existing model-free audio/mouth timing adapter separately on the same
tracked inputs and soundtrack mapping. Its windows and flags are context, not
the candidate's probability or proof of lip redraw. A good synchronization score
does not certify an authentic picture. Any passed screen still needs separately
frozen fresh actors and source sentences, evidence integration, source-position
correspondence, ordinary UI/PDF/JSON and paid-flow checks before local adoption.
The new statistic must retain zero overall-confidence weight until independently
calibrated for that purpose. A failed screen must not consume confirmation actors
to rescue it, and no outside review is implied by locally running these checks.
