# Source intake for July 2022 eLife Insights

The fixed cohort comprises all nine works listed as Insight in the July 2022 eLife Magazine archive, in archive order. The original publication dates precede the public launch of ChatGPT. Use the publisher's version-1 JATS XML from a single full Git commit. Require the expected DOI, article type, Insight category, volume, publication date and ordered author names. Verify each work's rights from its own JATS permissions. Admit only an explicit CC BY 4.0 link with the publisher license notice and no conflicting links. Retain the work's author names, title, source link and license attribution. The license permits adaptation and commercial reuse subject to attribution, including identifying later edits. [July archive](https://elifesciences.org/archive/2022/july) · [Publisher XML](https://github.com/elifesciences/elife-article-xml) · [Publisher terms](https://elifesciences.org/terms) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Use ordinary body paragraphs with their inline formatting rendered as text and their numbered references omitted. Omit images, captions, tables, headings and front or back matter. Drop paragraphs containing quote marks, external links, contact strings or unsupported markup. Gaps where prose was excluded break passage selection. Take the first contiguous eligible window of five to eight paragraphs that has 220–500 words and at least one interior paragraph of 24–120 words. From those interior paragraphs select one using the DOI suffix hash. Join the normalized paragraphs with two newlines and record each paragraph and the future rewrite target as half-open Unicode code point intervals. Record the raw XML, license notice, normalized body projection and selected excerpt hashes. A missing or rejected article retains its place in the nine-work denominator. No other article can fill it.

Check case-folded 12-token fragments from eligible article paragraphs and each selected excerpt against other candidates and prior project inputs. Replay the twenty v243 exposure packets with their sealed packet hashes and counts, the retained v257 OpAI-Bench cases reused by v260, the four v249 GOCE controls, and the English records in the hash-verified LLMTrace classification archive. The screen covers those known sources and exact normalized fragments. It leaves paraphrase similarity and pretraining exposure unmeasured. [Writing exposure inventory](text-goce-source-admission-v243-method.md) · [OpAI-Bench intake](text-opai-bench-intake-v257-method.md) · [GOCE passage comparison](text-goce-span-v249-method.md)

The command reads an owner-only local cache of the nine `elife-<id>-v1.xml` files obtained from the named publisher commit. A successful run prints source metadata, hashes, eligibility dispositions and overlap counts. With `--retain` it writes the exact admitted originals and a matching receipt under ignored `backend/data`. Only the public-safe receipt may be tracked. The intake makes no generator or detector calls. Any later generation or evaluation requires a separately frozen protocol and is limited to constructed science-explainer examples with documented publication provenance.

```sh
export PYTHONPATH=backend PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1
backend/.venv/bin/python -B -m scripts.text_elife_source_intake_v272 \
  /path/to/private/elife-xml-cache PUBLISHER_COMMIT /path/to/pinned/gohumanize/test.jsonl --retain
```
