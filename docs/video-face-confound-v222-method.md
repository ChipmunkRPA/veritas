# Model-free face-window confound measurements

This standalone, descriptive primitive accepts caller-supplied, already verified RGB frames and their tracked five landmarks, face boxes and ordered frame indices, grouped as before, during and after. The caller is responsible for verifying the source bytes, track binding, appearance-window role membership and native clock. Use exactly the measured crops' source frames; do not fill unsampled time positions. The primitive opens no media and invokes no models.

It compares the role medians of five-point configurations after removing translation, eye-line scale and in-plane rotation, reporting RMS geometry differences in inter-eye units. For photometry it takes a per-frame median inside the face bounding box and outside an expanded box, pools per role, and reports directional changes in RGB-derived luma and two chroma coordinates. Differences between face and non-face changes and their three-channel cosine describe alignment of the two shifts. The face box contains background and the non-face region is not motion-registered; moving cameras, changing backgrounds, expressions, shadows and face detection error can alter these statistics. RGB code values are not calibrated scene radiance. Coherent changes do not prove a codec or lighting cause, and face-specific changes do not prove an edit.

Require at least three distinct, ordered, nonoverlapping frames per role, valid finite RGB and geometry, adequate face and outside-box pixels, and a consistent frame size. Failure returns an explicit unavailable reason rather than a zero measurement. The only numeric cutoffs qualify minimum support and degenerate geometry; they are not decision thresholds. No result suppresses or changes an appearance interval, a customer verdict, or model confidence. Evaluation should retain availability and compare consumed controls and swaps separately before any prospective held-out study.
