# Surrounding-footage comparison: more locations, too many ordinary flags

**Reject both V10 combined arms under their frozen false-flag requirements.**
The primary combination located **21/32** known edits and the secondary
**26/32**, versus **14/32** for V8 absolute evidence. But ordinary-control
flags rose from **0/56** to **12/56** and **24/56**. Both arms passed the other
nine frozen gates, including first-family locations and retention of old
successes. More sensitivity does not justify this loss of specificity.
V8 remains the better retained research candidate; no new check is enabled.
Serving, global confidence, payment, old results and GCP are unchanged.

## What changed and what stayed frozen

The [predeclared protocol](mouth-context-v10-development.md) tests the middle
of a candidate interval against preceding/following footage, using the exact
V8 and V9 learned heads. It enumerates every eligible tracked window before
retaining12. The raw response is min(middle-before,middle-after), minus .25
times the before/after difference. Numerical centering reduces common-offset
cancellation without changing that algebra. This is a review statistic,
not a calibrated probability or causal pixel attribution.

Points were selected from actual ordinary **video-window** responses, rather
than maxima of separate still margins. The primary adds V9 relative evidence
only when V8 has no absolute flag; the secondary uses V8 relative evidence.
Both preserve the existing absolute flag/window as first evidence. Priority,
statistics, complete-panel gates and both heads/weights were frozen before new
responses. There was no post-result point or arm selection; neither arm passed.

The study reuses consumed subjects: 16 historical fitting actors, four separate
point actors and eight separate video-development actors. No weight training,
new media/model downloads or package changes. The primary entails two learned
heads; the secondary uses one. They share frozen backbone parameters, but this
research loads separately and makes no optimized-runtime or cloud speed claim.
Existing component permission declarations and CREMA-D ODbL/DbCL terms remain
separate; pretrained overlap/full training rights are not established here.

## Next target

Temporal comparison can expose first-family edits that absolute margins missed,
but it also responds to legitimate expression/motion. Next work needs explicit
ordinary-motion discrimination, trained and calibrated on complete video
windows with separate source groups. Full source-matched fit clips could
support a small temporal decision layer using context plus measured motion/
quality features; its design must be frozen before responses and compared
against the retained V8 reference. Do not silently raise/lower points on these
eight development actors or delete inconvenient control variants.

Any passing development still needs unseen actors AND new sentences, multiple
generators, realistic compression/resolution/ordinary edits, independent review,
source-linked UI/PDF/JSON/paid-flow integration and separate global-confidence
admission. GCP remains last. Public metadata contains no customer uploads,
media/checkpoint binaries or workstation paths.
