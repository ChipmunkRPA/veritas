The image gains above are localization results, not whole-file AI probabilities.

fresh validation. Sharing a backbone or agreeing on a response does not supply
independent corroboration. A raw map, an empty highlight or a skipped check must
not establish originality. Fitting/calibration, method selection and final
confirmation must use their assigned source roles. Unknown pretraining and
identity overlap remain explicit.
