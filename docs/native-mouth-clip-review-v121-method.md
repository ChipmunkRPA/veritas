requirements. Freeze before replay. Generation has begun, but no detector score
or achieved synchronization result is used to choose this audit. It is an
internal second implementation of difference/count checks, not an outside
person's independent review or a new detector experiment.

freeze commitment before reviewing that batch's36condition records. Preserve

achieved-synchronization flags. Retain unavailable conditions and their reasons;

the pre-generation native plan. For every ready output, rehash the media and
compare full picture PTS/time base/dimensions. Decode all output pictures.

bounding rectangle. Do not rerun a face detector or claim reconstruction of
the source mouth alpha from landmarks that this record does not retain.

signed-16 sample bytes, original audio origin and a continuous sample-source
mapping. Do not infer phonetic synchronization from waveform equality. The
