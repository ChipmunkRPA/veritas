# Shared video comparison: source bytes acquired

All **336 planned assets** have been acquired on the approved Seagate archive
and checked against immutable publisher byte counts and SHA-256 digests. A
separate sequential read rehashed every saved file. The total is 595,757,032
bytes across 144 provisional source groups. The [aggregate receipt](evaluation-panels/video-common-source-acquisition-v91.json)
contains whole-plan/profile hashes and execution measurements only.

The [source preparation](video-common-source-preparation-v89.md) remains the
governing metadata/history review. The panel includes 96 paired original and
two-method face-swap groups, plus 48 genuine audio/video actor sources. These
are source assets for a comparison, not 336 verified detection successes.
Portrait attribution/identity links absent from the publisher metadata remain
unknown; file names do not establish distinct people or complete ancestry.

Seven data-only batches completed without a failed batch or neural call.
Summed acquisition time was 285.43 seconds, with maximum per-read observed RSS
40,878,080 bytes and minimum observed available RAM 4,213,522,432 bytes. These
discrete observations do not establish continuous resource coverage or a true
peak. Source bytes and individual digests remain private.

Native frame timestamps, full decoded-content aliases and audio availability
still need qualification before assigning final study roles. Known edit
boundaries, ordinary controls and additional mouth manipulations are not yet
built. The common current/appearance/lip/synthetic-media comparison and its own
calibration have not started. Acquisition must not be reported as validation
of the app's video detection or of the recent papers' methods.
