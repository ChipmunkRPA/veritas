recipe. Predict the encoded clock from that recipe and explicitly label it
expected, not newly observed. A later native stage must bind the constructed
receipt, decoded RGB, actual clock/duration and second command review before

equal-distance ties toward the earlier index. Preserve each cadence, including
NTSC rational steps; do not convert everything to 25 Hz. Consecutive triplets

two-before and two-after center windows used in the v20 measurement protocol.

their measured differences. These are publisher/source labels, not exact

Retain source, planned-scope and publisher-provenance labels separately. Record
