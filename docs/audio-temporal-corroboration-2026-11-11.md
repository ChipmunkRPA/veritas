# Temporal corroboration of voice flags in strong background sound

This experiment asks whether an AI flag on one voice window survives two nearby measurements of the same continuous recording. It uses the existing classifier and operating points. It changes the time windows supplied to the model, while prior corroboration experiments changed the frequency content or representation of one window. [Earlier frequency corroboration](audio-frozen-corroboration-transfer-2026-11-11.md) · [Exact temporal results](evaluation-panels/audio-temporal-corroboration-2026-11-11.json)

## Fixed inputs and rule

Fourteen genuine English recordings come from different LibriSpeech readers. Their declared readers are absent from the exact serving calibration and evaluation manifest and the reviewed v81 and v169 roles. The source selection also excludes known audio and text identities. Thirteen Turkish recordings come from different VoxForge contributors under the archived GPL-3.0-or-later session grants. The synthetic controls contain ten designed Alania voices and ten Okur utterances from one stock voice. Earlier project exposure beyond these bindings and model pretraining overlap are unknown. [LibriSpeech release](https://www.openslr.org/12/) · [VoxForge rights review](audio-voxforge-tr-genuine-v192-method.md) · [Alania preparation](audio-alania-followup-v236-method.md) · [Okur preparation](audio-okur-turkish-v240-method.md)

An amplitude-only rule selects 3.5 seconds from each source before the voice model runs. It ranks fixed starts by the minimum number of active 20 ms frames across the three windows and requires at least 90 active frames per window. Within that interval, compare three overlapping three-second windows starting at 0.000, 0.250, and 0.500 seconds. Each speech recording has a gain-matched clean control and two mixtures with new rights-qualified MUSAN recordings, one at 0 dB and one at −5 dB whole-track SNR. Their measured power within 100–3,800 Hz is 97.4% and 63.8%, respectively. The two isolated backgrounds complete the 143 planned tracks. The audio and time coordinates are fixed before measuring speech activity. The first source qualification rejected all English readers under the broad historical catalogue exclusion. The final roster uses the known exact role exclusions and retains the earlier byte and text checks. This change preceded all model inference.

The production speech gate measures all three windows before authenticity scoring. Within each source lane and noise condition, select the fixed quota from clips with all three windows admitted, in salted hash order. Keep every planned and rejected track in the coverage record. The baseline reports the current central-window direction. For an existing AI flag, the new rule scores the two neighbors. It withholds the AI flag only when neither neighbor also leans AI. A withheld flag becomes inconclusive. No point is fitted or changed. The predeclared rule for a larger trial requires at least eight assessed genuine clips in both noise conditions, removal of false AI flags on at least two genuine groups and loss of at most one synthetic detection.

The current and temporal rules agree on all 60 scored views. Each condition scored six English and six Turkish genuine readers, four Alania voices, and four Okur utterances. Nineteen of 20 scored recordings are common to all three conditions. Every synthetic track passed speech admission in all three windows. Both isolated background controls remained unassessed. The broader genuine coverage makes this a stronger test of admission than the earlier two-background voice pooling study, which admitted only two and zero genuine views. The evaluated central windows still produced no genuine false positives for temporal corroboration to correct. [Earlier speech-pooling coverage](audio-speech-masked-representation-2026-11-11.md)

The rule did not meet its fixed advance criterion. The local voice score and customer confidence remain unchanged. The two noises differ in recording and level, and the overlapping speech windows share samples. These results describe continuous known-origin speech after the stated activity selection. They leave short voice replacement edits, natural mixtures, and a wider population of background sounds untested.

The speech gate made 40,326 actual frame forwards. The classifier made 134 forwards to score the selected central windows, the two corroborating windows where required, and existing stability probes. A separate readout verified every track and window hash, the 94 measured mixture residuals, exact preselection, source clocks, direction arithmetic, and model-call counts. The public artifact contains aggregate results and whole-artifact commitments. Recordings, transcripts, per-clip scores, and source identifiers stayed local.
