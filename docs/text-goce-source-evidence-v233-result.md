# Historical text evidence collected for four articles

The local collector obtained all ten exact article revisions, eight talk snapshots, 214 revision metadata entries and 96 earlier revision bodies across four candidate articles. It made 31 official API requests. Offline replay reproduced the complete aggregate with zero new requests. Three article metadata histories reached their first revision. The fourth article and all six earlier-wikitext trails reached their planned limits. These records support review of the seven candidate windows for original article prose, imported material and attribution. No rights decision or model score was recorded during collection. A local content review verified the exact render offsets in both versions of all seven windows. Six mapped uniquely to ordinary article-body paragraphs. The seventh included text produced by an unchanged unit-conversion template inside both rendered edit spans. That witness needs a narrower independently verified span before it could serve as an original-prose control. One source history also contains a page-to-page copy credit without an identified section, and a different source has linked talk archives outside the cached snapshots. These source questions remain open before benchmark admission.
