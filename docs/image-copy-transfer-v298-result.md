# Copy matching on eight licensed photos

The fixed cue recovered **24 of 32** deliberately copied regions across PNG and JPEG90. All 24 copy views that met the texture requirement recovered the correct donor and transform. The other eight lacked sufficient texture. The test processed all 80 planned views and reproduced the exact private readout locally.
