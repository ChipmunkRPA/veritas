# Supplemental audio diagnostics v86

This supplemental review is defined while v84 fitting/calibration extraction
is running, **before any selection or final-role neural forward**. It does not
change the frozen v84 models, sources, training, calibration, primary evaluation,
selection loss/margin or confirmation procedure. It is secondary descriptive
analysis, not a claim of having been fixed before the first fitting-data call.

Report every one of the 36 condition-by-source-family cells for every existing
predeclared method. Preserve empty cells and explicit inconclusive/unassessed
outcomes. Report both wrong directions, missed AI including unassessed cases,
new/lost correct cases versus current, physical source counts and connected
foreground-group counts. Do not infer independent sample support from repeated
conditions, group counts or shared backgrounds. There is no added admission,
zero-error or no-loss rule and no new model choice.

Review the exact frozen v81 source manifest and v82 background allocation
without looking at detector responses. Each role balances background use between
genuine and AI classes: 14 genuine and 15 AI foregrounds per background source.
Exposure is not perfectly balanced within each individual AI-generator family.
Publish anonymous sorted four-source count histograms by role/family/background
kind, including zeroes. Publish no source IDs, media or per-record pairings.
This is a nuisance-distribution limitation to disclose, not a reason to change
fixed inputs retrospectively or discard a useful paired comparison.

Interpret the v84 reliability outputs carefully. A source-kind cell contains
only one truth class. Its conditional Brier/log loss describes score errors for
that class; its equal-width error/bin summaries **cannot by themselves establish
or refute probability calibration**. Overall and condition-level reliability
bins include both truth classes and are the relevant descriptive calibration
views. Even those are subject to the declared reference prior, source mixture,
small group counts, shared backgrounds and known-source/training-overlap limits.
Do not present class-only ECE as an independent calibration failure/success.

Supplemental results must use the exact selected fitted artifacts and complete
recorded scalar predictions, preserving their input/artifact hashes. Primary
v84 selection remains immutable. Do not retune using a weak or favorable cell.
No new neural calls, real payment, serving/default change, workflow, outreach,
outside reviewer participation or GCP work is part of this reviewer.
