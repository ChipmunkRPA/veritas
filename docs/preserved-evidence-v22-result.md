problems:17 audio,32 actual PNG/JPEG image,16 video and37 passage problems.
They include all contexts from the passage study with recorded Linux failures.
Local/combination inputs were reconstructed with the original frozen models,
isolating the optimizer change. All102 fitted outputs passed a separate scalar-
summed objective/gradient feasibility check with KKT infinity residual<=1e-9.

The maximum original gradient residual is5.0557165e-9; maximum old/new training
logit drift is1.6345187e-7. The worst new objective excess is2.2204460e-16, within
the predeclared1e-11 rounding allowance. This repairs the numerical method for
further tests; it does not rewrite old successful/failed runs or prove universal
convergence. Source support, smoothing, priors, normalization, clipping and base
regularization are unchanged. Old v2/v3 and all serving models remain intact.

The initial v21 attempt reached the first fitted-problem diagnostic, then passed
