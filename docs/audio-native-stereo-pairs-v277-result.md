# Verified noisy-speech stereo benchmark

All **385 planned stereo WAVs** qualified locally. Development contains 185 cases from 37 foregrounds and holdout contains 200 cases from 40 separate foregrounds. Each foreground has one gain-matched clean file, two equal-gain background mixtures and two intentionally asymmetric mixtures. Development was replayed and sealed before the holdout stage started. [Frozen method](audio-native-stereo-pairs-v277-method.md) · [Frozen inputs and settings](evaluation-panels/audio-native-stereo-pair-freeze-v277.json) · [Development result](evaluation-panels/audio-native-stereo-pair-development-complete-v277.json) · [Holdout result](evaluation-panels/audio-native-stereo-pair-holdout-complete-v277.json)

Every case passed exact stereo PCM, production mono downmix, individual channel, and source-clock checks. The separate readouts reconstructed all 385 native cases and matched their saved bytes. The four DEMAND recording sessions were previously used on channel one. This benchmark uses different physical microphone channels and adds controlled mixtures of centered speech. Natural stereo speech and generalization to unseen background sessions are separate questions. Detector and channel-selection performance still need to be measured.
