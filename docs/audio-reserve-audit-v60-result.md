# An untouched recording panel is available for combination testing

The reserved v9 audio panel has 120 recordings in 118 connected foreground
groups and 1,080 prescribed acoustic variants. The recorded research audit found
no identity-matched detector outputs. This permits a separately frozen new
comparison of current and retained candidates; it does not change any earlier
failed experiment into a pass.

The panel contains 60 genuine recordings and 60 AI recordings: 24 ElevenLabs,
24 Chatterbox and 12 Kokoro. Each foreground has nine conditions, including
noise, music and overlapping speech. These are correlated variants, not 1,080
independent people. The original publisher terms remain applicable: CC BY 4.0,
CC BY-SA 4.0 and the recorded publisher MIT scenario declaration. No new media
or speech features are published here.

The audit followed moved research directories and examined 4,802 JSON/JSONL
files (2,660,483,796 bytes; 329,336 parsed objects). The 8,682 identity-matched
records in 24 files were acquisition metadata, source plans, identity audits
and waveform reconstruction receipts. None carried the searched measured
screening fields. Plans and decoded waveform checks are not model inference.

An additive review normalizes PCM field aliases and declared text, rather than
treating absent literal field names as evidence of separation. All 120 reserve
rows have byte, PCM, declared voice, full-text and eight-word-prefix identity
coverage. The 472 consumed rows have byte/PCM/voice coverage; 368 have declared
text coverage. No known connection crosses those reserve/consumed roles. The
additional PCM-alias scan found no recorded screening outputs and reverified
all 4,802 file hashes. Missing consumed transcripts remain a limitation.

Some reserve voices appeared in older project studies. This is an untouched
recording panel, not an entirely unseen-voice or unseen-pretraining benchmark.
Recorded metadata cannot prove absence of unrecorded calls or biometric overlap.
The earlier source-identity review and its historical voice-reuse disclosures
remain in force. No model calls, fitting or serving changes occurred in this
audit.

The next prospective comparison should retain all fixed source cases and all
outcomes, use the already fitted independent calibrations without refitting,
and compare standalone heads and actual decision combinations on the same
inputs. Report newly recovered and lost correct cases, false AI, false original,
inconclusive and unassessed results by generator and acoustic condition. A
modest increase in false calls is a tradeoff to assess, not an automatic veto.
Any selected combined probability still needs its own independent calibration
before entering customer confidence.
