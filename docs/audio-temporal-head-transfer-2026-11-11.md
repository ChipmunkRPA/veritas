## Frozen method

The classifier takes a masked mean over valid encoder steps and then applies an affine head. This lets the evaluation apply the same head to each encoder step of one forward. The sigmoid of the mean of those per-step logits must reproduce the ordinary raw response within the frozen numerical tolerance. Average the logits within nonoverlapping, complete 20-step blocks, about 400 ms per block, and retain the second-highest block mean. A lone peak cannot satisfy this rule. The encoder sees the entire input, so these scores are correlated and do not establish localized authorship or edit boundaries. Work on short partial spoofs and temporal pooling motivates testing whether time variation is useful. This study uses the current commercially reviewed checkpoint and does not import code or weights from those papers.
