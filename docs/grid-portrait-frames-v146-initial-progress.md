repeat. All 80 native composites changed pixels only inside their intended mouth

RSS **2.31 GiB** and no guard or sampling failure. The 900-second/6-GiB neural
limits and running reserves were unchanged. See the
[initial actual receipt](evaluation-panels/grid-portrait-frames-initial-progress-v146.json)
and [frozen method](grid-portrait-frames-v146-method.md).

not measure achieved speech motion, identity face swaps or detector accuracy.
