# Condition-specific operating points for voice screening

The previous competing-speech test found that the current voice score ranked many known synthetic clips above genuine clips without issuing an AI direction. Earlier severe mixtures also produced substantial genuine false flags. This comparison holds the detector and its raw score fixed and measures whether an acoustic condition can support a different internal operating point. ## Frozen fit and transfer

Use 102 fitting and 54 point-check views, partitioned by original foreground and background. Three earlier matched-babble components and two stereo scenes supply fitting. The fourth matched-babble component and two separate stereo coherence scenes supply the point check. Keep all variants from a foreground in the same role. Use archived raw responses and the current direction for the exact signed 16-bit, 16 kHz mono waveform. The selected point and acoustic rule are fixed before opening either transfer. [Matched babble construction](audio-matched-babble-v181-result.md) · [Stereo fitting scenes](audio-stereo-component-transfer-2026-11-11.md) · [Stereo point-check scenes](audio-natural-stereo-coherence-2026-11-11.md)

For each exact three-second input, compute RMS in all 150 non-overlapping 20 ms frames. Measure the contrast between its 80th and 20th percentile frame RMS levels. A lower contrast describes a persistently audible mixed signal. The cue becomes unavailable when the 20th percentile is zero or the upper percentile falls below −45 dBFS. Choose the contrast on fitting scenes alone, maximizing mean sensitivity to added speech while keeping at least 80% of each clean fitting source collection outside the route. The chosen contrast was 12 dB. This is an observable property of the mixed PCM. It supplies no SNR estimate, speaker count, or origin claim.

Fit one internal raw-score point for windows inside that acoustic route. Compare the same procedure applied to every admitted window to measure the effect of routing. Both preserve original and unassessed directions. The point can only change AI and inconclusive directions. Search the predeclared raw grid on fitting scenes. Require unchanged clean directions, at most one new genuine false flag and at most one lost correct synthetic flag per fit scene. Prefer the best net change in correct decisions and keep the current decision if no candidate improves fitting. The separate point role records transfer without refitting. Advance only if the routed rule passes the point check and yields at least two net correct changes on the fresh noisy scenes with unchanged clean decisions, zero additional genuine flags and at most one lost synthetic flag per scene. The independently replayed high-false archive must also add no genuine false flags. ## Local results

The routed candidate removed 16 false flags and lost one true flag on fitting. It removed five false flags and lost one true flag on separate point-check scenes. The all-admitted comparator removed 23 and eight false flags, respectively, at a cost of one true flag in each role. Neither added false flags or changed a clean direction in either role. Both selected the same stricter internal raw point.

The two proposed policies produced identical directions across all 80 fresh views. The first noise scene suppressed two Alania, one Okur, and two Drifting true flags. The second suppressed one Alania, three Okur, and three Drifting flags. The higher-overlap scene had 930 ms of simultaneous annotated background words, and the other had zero. The noise-only control for the first scene was unassessed, and the other was inconclusive. Both stayed unchanged. The acoustic rule routed six of 25 assessed clean foregrounds, all 23 assessed foregrounds in the first noisy scene, and 19 of 26 in the second. Routing was too broad to retain any of the noisy flags that the all-admitted comparator lost.

The current raw score put every synthetic clip above all assessed genuine clips in the single-interferer scene. Seven of 14 cleared that rank in the overlapping-interferer scene. The corresponding raw ROC AUCs were 1.000 and 0.875. These sample-specific ranks show why applying one stricter point after a loudness-contrast measurement can lose useful decisions across distinct scenes. They supply no new operating point for uploads.

In the predeclared English archive, both proposals suppressed four genuine false flags and two synthetic true flags in background A. In background B they suppressed one false and one true flag. Clean directions were unchanged. The stored archive verifies a real reduction from five false flags to zero at a cost of three of 15 correct noisy AI flags. The source scenes are different from the fresh competing-speech test and their baseline outcomes were already known.

The frozen rule failed fresh advancement. It withheld 12 of 13 true noisy flags while removing one false flag. The voice calibration and customer confidence remain unchanged. The run made 7,520 speech-gate frame forwards and 85 classifier forwards on 75 assessed inputs. The separate readout reproduced the fixed points, all 80 exact waveforms and acoustic measurements, the 52 measured background residuals and all reported directions. The archived stress transfer made zero new neural calls. Raw samples, per-file scores and source identifiers remain local.
