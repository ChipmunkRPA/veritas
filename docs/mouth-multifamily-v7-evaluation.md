## Native construction and source audit

Every original contributes five variants: ordinary matched resampling, Gaussian
blur at sigma 2 and 4 native pixels, nominally audio-aligned neural redraw, and
redraw conditioned eight frames earlier. All variants share the same native
mouth ellipse, feathering and centred 20-frame interval. Blur controls account
for softness seen in the engineering output. Both neural cases have known AI
provenance; requested conditioning is not a measured achieved sync offset.

All 40 planned clips were prepared without substitution. Lossless encoding
preserved the intended arrays, PTS and original PCM. The separate
[source audit](evaluation-panels/mouth-v7-source-neural-audit.json) passed:

- Every frame of all clips: **2,740 native decoded frames** and exact clocks.
- All **800 changed-pixel masks**, original surrounding frames, freshly detected
  support/crop geometry and unchanged soundtracks.
- Exact reconstruction of all three ordinary operations, including edge opacity.
- All **320 neural image/audio/seed input bindings**.
- **32 of 320 neural output forwards**, using the first and eleventh edited
  frame of both neural cases for every actor. Recorded intermediate proofs and
  final native composites reproduced bitwise on the same MPS runtime.

The last check is sampled, not a claim of rerunning every neural output. These
are local source/operation checks, not outside review or unknown-media proof.
The [complete input manifest](evaluation-panels/mouth-v7-development-inputs.json)
retains positions, masks, transforms and per-render proof hashes; no media or
checkpoint binary is published. Its `source_sha256` refers to the v4 lossless
parent, `upstream_normalized_source_sha256` to the earlier normalized recording,
and `source_url` to the raw publisher original. Use the v4 source catalogue to
resolve those separate byte identities; they are not interchangeable hashes.
