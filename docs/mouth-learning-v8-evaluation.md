**0/32** for the unchanged current cue. It flagged **0/56 ordinary controls**,
versus **1/56**. All 88 executions and baseline/native-input replays passed.
However, all 14 successes were in the MuseTalk family; **0/16 LivePortrait

**Do not enable this candidate or advance it to fresh confirmation as a passing
replacement.** Retain its learned state and evidence as research. Serving

All actors and sentence families were consumed previously. This is not a fresh
or independent accuracy study; derived variants are correlated.

Source clocks matter: the original training recordings can start picture one
25 Hz tick after the audio. Generation matches each picture's actual PTS to
the contiguous decoded audio origin, rather than equating frame index with
conditioning index. Nominally aligned/eight-tick-earlier conditioning records
the requested input, not achieved synchronization. The permitted v7 generator,
replacement geometry, pinned assets and notices are unchanged. Source footage
retains CREMA-D ODbL/DbCL terms; MIT/Apache-2.0 declarations for components do
not establish complete training rights or unknown pretrained disjointness.

The DINOv2-small embeddings/first ten blocks stayed frozen. **Blocks 10/11,
final layer normalization and a 768-to-1 head actually learned**, totaling
3,552,001 parameters. Fit-only source/class/condition weights, native/flipped/
JPEG75/flipped-JPEG75 views and seed 1729 followed the locked schedule. All 12
epochs ran; the final state was used without checkpoint selection. Weighted
fitting BCE changed from 0.693331 in epoch 1 to 0.437550 in epoch 12, with
non-monotone later epochs. Training loss is not validation accuracy. All frozen
parameter hashes remained unchanged. The maximum ordinary native margin on the
four point actors set the separate raw review point, **2.117572976827109**;
