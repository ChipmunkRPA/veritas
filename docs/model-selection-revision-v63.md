combinations on common inputs before choosing. A small increase in false alarms
or any lost correct case is not an automatic rejection.

## How the revised comparisons choose

1. **Keep several plausible candidates.** Compare individual checks, wider
   combinations, shared-evidence combinations and quality-aware routing where
   justified. Routing may use measured input quality and coverage; it must not
   use the known answer, generator label or test-set annotation as a predictor.
2. **Use the same examples.** Preserve all planned cases, failures and skipped
   checks. Report recovered and lost correct cases, both wrong directions,
   inconclusive and unassessed outcomes, runtime and memory. Use connected
   source groups rather than counting correlated frames/windows as people.
3. **Measure the right evidence.** Compare actual pixel masks, face/time
   intervals and character offsets. An overlapping rectangle is not agreement
   on the pixels inside it. Background continuity is editing context; a voice
   classifier does not identify which overlapping speaker is synthetic.
4. **Judge explicit tradeoffs.** Retain more than one useful operating choice
   when sensitivity, false alarms, coverage and cost differ. Assess the size
   and distribution of gains and errors; do not impose a zero-loss rule or
   choose only a favorable subgroup. Describe uncertainty and known dependence.
5. **Fit and calibrate combinations separately.** Each candidate keeps its own
   calibration. Fit combinations on their assigned fitting sources, calibrate
   each on separate calibration sources, compare the fixed candidates on method-
   selection sources, then confirm the chosen method on a final separate role.
   Agreement, a larger union or an average of confidence scores does not
   automatically create a calibrated probability. Do not repeatedly tune on
   final answers or refit the chosen maps using their confirmation responses.
6. **Adopt useful improvements after serving checks.** Confirm preprocessing,
   evidence geometry/time/text alignment, UI/PDF/JSON consistency, charge-before-
   analysis behavior and rollback locally. A useful improvement may be adopted
   with disclosed tradeoffs; perfect classification is not required. GCP comes
   after this work.

Commercial permissions and faithful implementation remain requirements, distinct
from an experimental performance gate. Read noncommercial research for ideas
without incorporating restricted weights or code. Record weight/data provenance
and unknown training overlap. Only trusted, exact-file-bound explicit-AI
provenance can justify stopping statistical checks; absent or unverifiable tags
do not establish originality.

## Earlier provenance and fusion assumptions corrected

The optional older pipeline still treated editable generator fields as decisive,
assigned arbitrary authenticity probabilities to camera fields and parsed
credentials, and described its hand-weighted log-odds average as calibrated.
Those assumptions are now removed. Its metadata reader preserves the observed
fields and explanations without an authorship probability or statistical
weight. The common fallback also excludes metadata/provenance records from its
score, so old or independently supplied decisive flags cannot bypass the
trusted provenance gate. The score copy identifies hand-set weighting and
signal-agreement confidence without claiming empirical calibration or model
independence. No neural weight or calibrated research method changed.

Eighteen focused regressions cover actual PNG fields, copied camera fields,
parsed credential states, tool names without AI actions, and forged decisive
flags in both directions. The broader current provenance tests also passed,
including actual signed-asset checks: the staged trusted explicit-AI gate keeps
working while absent, unsigned, unbound or ambiguous records do not stop content
checks. These are local software/correctness checks, not new detector accuracy
measurements or an outside review. The current default staged pipeline remains
selected, and the frozen audio study bindings were reverified unchanged.

One remaining generated voice-reference sentence still exposed the retired
80% service-threshold wording. The public projection now replaces that exact
application sentence with scope and uncertainty wording, including in saved
results. Source material and measured percentages remain intact. Local public
projection/history and customer export checks pass; numerical detector code,
calibration bytes and experiment bindings remain unchanged.

Customer-facing findings continue to name the checked scope and supporting
evidence without model identities or a public threshold claim. Skipped and
