# Crossref citation snapshot

Chipmunk Verify selected these public bibliographic facts from the Crossref
REST works endpoint on November 18, 2026. Crossref describes bibliographic
metadata as facts outside copyright and releases its own generated metadata
under CC0. The snapshot uses published identifiers, title alternatives, first
recorded family names and DOI-to-DOI version or format relationships.
[Crossref metadata terms](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/)

The bounded selection contains 364 works returned for 370 distinct DOI
lookups from public references in the local evaluation. DOI keys and relation
targets are case-folded. The title projection preserves the registered title
wording after removing presentation markup and normalizing whitespace. Family
names identify eligible journal articles, proceedings articles and posted
content. A `null` family leaves a record available for DOI resolution and
destination-title comparison. Some relationship targets fall outside the
lookup set. [Evaluation and local recount](../../../../docs/writing-doi-title-verification-2026-11-18.md)

The JSON is a dated collection for offline comparison. Its availability and
the metadata deposited by publishers bound what an observation can establish.
The same date and bytes identify the snapshot on replay.
