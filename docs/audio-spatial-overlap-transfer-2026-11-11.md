# Stereo arrival-time evidence for overlapping speech

This trial measures whether a stereo timing cue can identify simultaneous speech and help withhold unreliable AI voice flags. An earlier learned overlap rule withheld 23 false flags and 31 correct synthetic flags. The new cue uses relative arrival times in the original two microphone channels. It leaves the audio samples unchanged. [Earlier competing-speech trial](audio-babble-overlap-comparison-v183-result.md) · [Exact aggregate](evaluation-panels/audio-spatial-overlap-transfer-2026-11-11.json)

## Fixed method

Use the first-party AMI microphone recordings and v1.6.2 manual word timings, distributed under CC BY 4.0. Two earlier meeting sessions supply 32 acoustic development windows. Two newly acquired sessions supply 32 confirmation windows. In each session choose eight windows with at most 100 ms of simultaneous annotated words and eight with at least 900 ms. All contain at least 2.1 seconds of annotated words from one or more participants. Join words within one speaker over gaps up to 80 ms and count occupancy in 10 ms cells. Select non-overlapping three-second windows from four-second grid starts by salted hash order. Manual word ranges provide a repeatable label. They can miss breaths, nonverbal speech and other sounds. [AMI official downloads and license](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/download/) · [Capture and clock notes](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/signals.shtml) · [Earlier source acquisition](audio-stereo-component-transfer-2026-11-11.md)

On 1,024-sample Hann frames with a 512-sample hop, estimate the phase-correlation peak separately in six frequency bands between 300 Hz and 4.4 kHz. Keep usable band peaks on the fixed half-sample delay grid. A frame supports the spatial cue when at least two bands agree near each of two arrival delays separated by at least 3.5 samples. The per-window cue is the fraction of eligible frames with both delay groups. Fewer than 24 eligible frames leaves the cue unavailable. Select the cutoff on the development meetings alone, with at most two false markers per eight minimal-overlap windows in either meeting. The cutoff was 0.08. The model-free calculation can also respond to reflections or channel effects. [Generalized cross-correlation](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1162830) · [Frequency-band time-delay method](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08838)

For matched synthetic controls, add 12 fixed three-second utterances from three generator families to one recorded background per confirmation session, at −5 dB mono SNR. The background comes from a minimal-overlap native window. The added voice enters both channels equally. Each utterance has a gain-matched clean control and two noisy variants. The four Drifting utterances share two stock voice groups and appeared in an older kitchen and traffic study. The Okur utterances share one stock voice. These fixed control mixes test suppression of real synthetic flags under interference. [Alania source review](audio-alania-followup-v236-method.md) · [Okur source review](audio-okur-turkish-followup-v245-method.md) · [Drifting source review](audio-drifting-turkish-v215-method.md)

Use the current frozen speech admission and classifier calibration. The context-only rule can change an AI flag to inconclusive when the spatial cue is present. The primary rule also requires both untouched physical channels to pass speech admission and at least one channel to return an inconclusive or original direction. The physical channels share recording content and the same classifier. Each three-second result remains a single assessment. Advancement requires detecting at least half of annotated overlap in confirmation, retaining at least six of eight minimal-overlap controls without a marker per room, correcting at least one genuine false flag in each room, and losing at most one synthetic AI flag.

## Local results

The cue marked three of 16 annotated overlap windows in confirmation. All three came from one meeting. The other 13 overlap windows and all 16 minimal-overlap controls were unavailable under the frame-support rule. Zero markers among the minimal-overlap controls therefore gives no measured specificity. The frozen acoustic and decision advancement criteria failed.

The current route returned seven original and 11 inconclusive directions across the 18 assessed genuine windows. Fourteen native windows were unassessed. The context-only policy withheld 15 of 17 noisy true flags, and channel corroboration withheld six. Neither policy returned original for any synthetic control. The new native cohort had no genuine false flag for either rule to correct.

A separate retrospective replay used the already scored matched genuine and synthetic controls from the preceding stereo experiment. It evaluated the fixed cue on all 32 exact stereo mixtures and reused their current mono directions. In the first scene it would have withheld none of two genuine false flags or five synthetic true flags. In the second it would have withheld all six genuine false flags and all four synthetic true flags. Every tested mixture in that scene received the spatial cue. This indicates a scene-wide limitation rather than selective identification of detector errors. The replay made zero new classifier calls.
