rigid admission rule rejected them. Preserve the old experiments and their

combinations on common inputs before choosing. A small increase in false alarms
or any lost correct case is not an automatic rejection.

## How the revised comparisons choose

1. **Keep several plausible candidates.** Compare individual checks, wider
   combinations, shared-evidence combinations and quality-aware routing where
   justified. Routing may use measured input quality and coverage; it must not
   use the known answer, generator label or test-set annotation as a predictor.
2. **Use the same examples.** Preserve all planned cases, failures and skipped
   checks. Report recovered and lost correct cases, both wrong directions,
   inconclusive and unassessed outcomes, runtime and memory. Use connected
   source groups rather than counting correlated frames/windows as people.
3. **Measure the right evidence.** Compare actual pixel masks, face/time
   intervals and character offsets. An overlapping rectangle is not agreement
   on the pixels inside it. Background continuity is editing context; a voice
   classifier does not identify which overlapping speaker is synthetic.
4. **Judge explicit tradeoffs.** Retain more than one useful operating choice
   when sensitivity, false alarms, coverage and cost differ. Assess the size
   and distribution of gains and errors; do not impose a zero-loss rule or
   choose only a favorable subgroup. Describe uncertainty and known dependence.
5. **Calibrate after selecting the method.** Each model keeps its own valid
   calibration. A combination needs a calibration fitted for that combination
   on separately assigned sources. Agreement, a larger union or an average of
   confidence scores does not automatically create a calibrated probability.
   Preserve separate final confirmation; do not repeatedly tune on its answers.
6. **Adopt useful improvements after serving checks.** Confirm preprocessing,
   evidence geometry/time/text alignment, UI/PDF/JSON consistency, charge-before-
   analysis behavior and rollback locally. A useful improvement may be adopted
   with disclosed tradeoffs; perfect classification is not required. GCP comes
   after this work.

Commercial permissions and faithful implementation remain requirements, distinct
from an experimental performance gate. Read noncommercial research for ideas
without incorporating restricted weights or code. Record weight/data provenance
and unknown training overlap. Only trusted, exact-file-bound explicit-AI
provenance can justify stopping statistical checks; absent or unverifiable tags
do not establish originality.

Customer-facing findings continue to name the checked scope and supporting
evidence without model identities or a public threshold claim. Skipped and
inconclusive checks cannot become an original-file verdict. The automated SaaS
report remains preliminary screening; private expert work requires a separate
engagement through sales@chipmunkrpa.com.

finished. No outside reviewer participation, workflow run or deployment is
