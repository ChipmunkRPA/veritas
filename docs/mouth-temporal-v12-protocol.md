## Fixed learner and selection

Use one binary HistGradientBoostingClassifier: log loss, learning rate.05,
100 iterations, at most seven leaves/depth three, minimum forty examples per
leaf, L2 regularization1, sixty-four bins, no early stopping or categorical
features, seed2718. Other implementation defaults are bound by the installed
source and exact library version. Keep one OpenMP and one OpenBLAS thread.
These limits constrain a nonlinear interaction model on only sixteen fitting
actors; they do not establish that its capacity is appropriate in deployment.

Use all and only the original fitting windows and their original
actor/class/condition sample weights. Keep finite raw features without a
normalization fit. Source identity, generator, condition, role and truth do not
enter inference variables. A positive fitting window retains the prior
AI-clip, temporal-IoU>=.2 and representative-mask-overlap definition. It is
supervision from authored changes, not ground-truth human lip-sync perception.

pickle or missing/categorical fallback. Verify exported raw signed margins
