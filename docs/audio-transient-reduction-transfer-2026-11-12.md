## Fixed comparison

The candidate uses a 512-sample Hann transform and 128-sample hop. For
each point, compare median magnitude across 17 time frames with median
magnitude across 17 frequency bins. Retain the horizontal power fraction,
with an amplitude floor of 0.5. Invert using the original complex phase
and sample count. This is a conservative projection inspired by harmonic
and percussive separation. It can retain sustained noise and attenuate
speech consonants. It does not isolate the speaker or identify the source
of any sound. The implementation uses standard signal-processing operations
and the installed licensed voice and speech-activity assets. [Median
separation method](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/details/DsmIVcydPX66AuaqEmKyTQ)
· [Installed asset terms](commercial-model-audit.md)

Revisit the same inconclusive and unassessed three-second windows used in
the separate frequency-view comparison. Recheck speech activity after
processing. Set the operating point to the largest admitted selection
score and apply it strictly. A new interval requires two consecutive
AI-like windows, with at least one new projected response. Preserve all
focused intervals. The 25 selection and 98 held files are the exact prior
recordings. They had previous project exposure and no candidate filter
