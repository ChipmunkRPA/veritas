# Fresh shared-noise baseline scoring, v219

This prospective scoring continuation consumes the exact guarded and published v217 preparation: 16 fixed ordinal triplets, each with genuine Turkish, Alania, and Drifting arms, under matched clean and four shared kitchen/traffic noise conditions. It uses the unchanged frozen speech route and assets. The source preparation's receipt, code, roster, data guard, saved waveforms, and shared noise commitments are verified before the serving identity is admitted. The scorer does not import the source preparation or its app configuration. Preparation-unavailable arms remain in the planned roster.

Five serial guarded offline batches each score one condition across the 48 planned arms. Every earlier batch must have a complete verified receipt before the next can start. Private observations retain individual outputs and model-call accounting. The aggregate reports, per arm and condition, planned, scored, speech-gate rejected and preparation-unavailable counts, direction counts, and false AI for genuine or wrong-original and missed AI flags over the full planned count for generated audio. A missed AI flag includes inconclusive and unavailable outcomes, not just wrong-original decisions. Clean-to-noise transitions include route-status transitions and both-scored counts; coverage reports matched cross-arm pairs and complete triplets.

The cohorts and recorded tapes are limited samples and source class is confounded with cohort. This measures neither population performance nor a general effect of noise. No calibration, serving default, or holdout access is authorized. Public aggregate results omit case identities, timestamps, and model identity. Do not publish private rows or waveforms.
