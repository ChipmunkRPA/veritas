## Fitting and independent calibration

Two predeclared arms: a shared source classifier combined through an any-source
noisy-OR bag response, and that bag response combined with a nonnegative original
mixture raw-logit coefficient and intercept. Both arms retain the original
mixture assessment scope, so the separated arm is not a complete independent
service. AI-mixture labels supervise a bag containing two source estimates;
they are never copied to both estimates as source-level AI truth.

The source response uses its own raw logit, standardized embedding and intercept.
The stable bag logit is `logsumexp(z1, z2, z1 + z2)`, invariant to source
permutation. Conditional independence here is a modeling form; it does not
establish that estimates or artifacts are independent. Per-source responses
are not individually calibrated authorship probabilities.

Fit only original fitting roles, weighted equally by truth, generator within
truth, connected group, original source and assessed variants. Each estimated
source receives half its bag weight when fitting the shared embedding center
and population standard deviation. Use floor `1e-6`, variance budget 16 over
1,024 embedding dimensions. Preserve nonnegative explicit source and mixture
raw slopes; unconstrained embeddings do not imply physical monotonicity.
Representation ridge is 0.05, explicit raw ridge 0.001, intercept ridge zero.
Use connected truth-group target smoothing and one fixed initial solution.
L-BFGS-B uses `ftol=1e-12`, `gtol=1e-9`, maximum 1,000 iterations. This objective
is nonconvex; local convergence is not evidence of global optimality.

After fitting is saved immutably, use only original independent calibration
roles for each arm's worst nonconformity by connected group and truth. A group
with an unassessed member retains its worst-case missingness; it cannot become
an easy genuine calibration example. No condition/generator/source identity or
known clean reference enters the predictors or inference-time routing. No
validation-based tuning, threshold search or optimizer restart selection.

For an original-leaning candidate proposal, rerun the original fixed band view
on the source mixture, rerun the separator, and re-extract both source features.
Require both candidate and original raw log-odds stability at most `ln(2)`,
with the existing six-decimal probability treatment. Reuse a known original
filtered response only after its exact PCM hash matches; otherwise run it
through the unchanged voice model. Missing/failed required probes prevent an
original decision. The original-model loader owns inter-op initialization once
per process; the new CLI initializes only the declared intra-op thread count.

case, false AI, false original, AI without an AI conclusion, inconclusive and
correct conclusions, probability errors, connected-group uncertainty and every

3. Upper connected-group Brier interval below zero.
4. Lower Brier in each original strong condition.
5. At most one additional false call in each direction overall.
6. At most one additional false call in either direction per condition.
7. At most one additional false-original call per generator.
8. Lose at most two correct calls per truth class on clean input.
9. Lose at most two correct AI calls per generator.
10. Gain at least five correct calls for both noise0 and music5.
11. Gain at least five correct calls over background-speech variants.
12. Lose at most two correct calls in either truth class at each speech level.
13. Preserve exactly the originally assessed cases.
14. Complete the entire source/condition matrix.
15. No extraction failures.
16. No probe failures.

All sixteen must pass. Prefer the separated arm when both pass unless the
complement adds at least five correct calls, increases neither wrong direction
and has no worse Brier error. A passing consumed result permits only a separately
frozen fresh comparison; it never automatically enables serving or confidence.
Source-recovery measurements using known construction components are optional
descriptive diagnostics, not additional predictors or post-result selection gates.
