float32 state, inspected source graph, constant position buffer and eager
attention verified in preparation. Run on CPU with two threads. Preserve the
publisher's stride and center-window rule: eight actual pictures from the first

No source truth, dataset, generator or original reference enters either

## Calibration, outcomes and comparison

For each dataset, use only its original calibration role, both formats together,
and the common rows both methods can assess. Require both truth classes and at
least eight common independent calibration groups. Fit each method separately
with the unchanged reviewed video Platt implementation: clipped raw logits
[-20,20], equal truth-class weights at reference prior0.5, nonnegative slope,
ridge0.01*(slope-1)^2, L-BFGS-B starting at[1,0]. No parameter search or restart
selection. Common applicability can select a biased calibration subset; report
its size/groups and preserve every out-of-scope case in all-source counts.

The old internal research references0.8/0.2 are frozen solely for directional
comparison. They are not a public service threshold or promised accuracy.
Intermediate, absent, unsupported and unavailable scores are inconclusive.
Raw candidate probabilities are not calibrated customer confidence.

Report each dataset/format's paired rank/probability errors, mean Brier and log
loss differences, source-group bootstrap intervals, coverage, false AI, false
original, AI without an AI conclusion, correct conclusions and inconclusive
counts. Keep all192 planned development variants. Bootstrap2,000 times with
seed901, preserving source groups; variants and camera/generated action pairs
are correlated. Intervals condition on fitted calibration/selected sources and
do not include pretraining, calibration-fit or population uncertainty.

Every requirement must pass before fresh confirmation:

1. Complete exact288-input journals for both methods.
2. No unexpected decode, source-integrity or model failures. Unsupported scope
   and the four known unavailable inputs remain distinct and visible.
3. Both classes and at least eight common calibration groups per dataset.
4. Paired mean Brier gain of at least0.005 in every dataset/format.
5. Upper paired Brier interval below zero in every dataset/format.
6. At most one additional false AI and one additional false original, both
   overall and within each dataset/format.
7. At least four additional distinct AI originals with an AI conclusion in
   some format; lose at most two correct AI conclusions per dataset/format.
8. Lose at most two correct original conclusions overall or per dataset/format.
9. More correct directional conclusions overall.
10. Every baseline-assessed input remains assessed by the candidate.
11. All sixteen preselected native replays pass, including genuinely unassessed

These are deliberately fixed development requirements, not a statement that
the method is better or a license to retune after failures. Preserve and reject
a failed result. No source-disjoint confirmation is permitted unless all pass.
