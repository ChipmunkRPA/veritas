# Speech admission with a measured stereo channel choice

The development comparison admitted **175 of 185** clips when it allowed the selected channel to supplement mono, up from **168 of 185** with mono alone. All seven additional admissions came from the intentionally asymmetric mixtures. They included three genuine and four expressive synthetic speech clips. The equal-gain mixtures added no admissions. The method selected a channel in 48 of 74 forced-imbalance cases and chose the known quieter side in all 48. Swapping the channel order preserved every selection or abstention. The rule used speech activity and acoustic contrast for channel choice. All 52,170 model forwards measured speech activity. A follow-up scored authenticity on the seven recovered clips and eligible comparisons. The 200 holdout cases stayed closed.
