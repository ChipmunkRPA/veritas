# Independent voice screening under loud backgrounds

This trial asks whether a separate voice detector can recover some current inconclusive results when recorded speech or traffic is louder than the foreground. The original waveform, speech-admission gate and current calibration remain the comparison. Earlier enhancement and second-view experiments added genuine false flags under traffic. ## Detector and decision rule

Use the 364 MB CPU INT8 Spectra-AASIST3 derivative at pinned revision `b56aed04853cb4e5bf825025c54c93d4bc345c61`. The derivative publisher records the exact SHA-256 of the Apache-2.0 base model and its weight-only MatMul conversion. The base model uses an XLS-R-300M speech encoder and a spectral-temporal graph attention head, separate from the current voice detector. The base checkpoint, the quantized release and the XLS-R base model are all marked Apache-2.0 by their publishers. This test evaluates the exact CPU derivative. The original FP32 scores and benchmark claims do not establish this release’s behavior under our acoustic conditions. Training-source overlap could not be measured. Both detectors receive the same admitted three-second, 16 kHz mono interval. The candidate’s published preprocessing applies 0.97 pre-emphasis to that interval, then repeats its initial samples to reach the graph’s 64,600-sample input. A reported source interval stays at three seconds. A repeated sample is part of inference preparation and supplies no additional source coverage. The independent spoofing score is the negative of the bona-fide logit.

The operating point is the highest candidate score among incumbent-inconclusive genuine or background-only cases in a separate cohort. Strictly exceeding that point can change only an incumbent-inconclusive direction. A direct raw-score comparator uses the same point selection on the incumbent response. Candidate-alone AI flags are also counted on the shared admitted set. Existing AI, original and unassessed outcomes are preserved in both combined policies. These diagnostic scores do not estimate a customer probability.

The advancement gate requires new synthetic flags from at least three groups and two generator families, at least one gain in each confirmation room, no more than one added genuine false flag overall, zero added clean and isolated background flags, and at least six assessed genuine examples per confirmation scene.

## Local recovery result

The operating points were committed from 28 eligible views across 15 genuine groups. The candidate recovered zero previously inconclusive synthetic views and added zero genuine false flags. Its single standalone AI flag was already caught by the incumbent. The incumbent raw-score comparator added three genuine false flags and zero correct flags. The frozen advancement test failed.
