## Complete fitting construction

All sixteen original fitting actors were retained, separate from four point
actors and eight consumed development actors. Each source supplies original,
lighting, two resampling, two blur and four neural-edit conditions, for160
clips. The two existing permitted generator families supply same-frame/
earlier-aperture and aligned/earlier-audio construction variants. These labels
do not prove that the requested speech synchronization was achieved.

Every clip uses its complete exact common source span,51–71 pictures, at25Hz
with the corresponding original16kHz PCM. Picture/audio origin differences are
trimmed explicitly; there is no padding, repetition or replacement audio.
All operations occupy a centred20-frame interval, with two-frame edge opacity.
Lossless FFV1/PCM encoding preserves native evidence and resets the clip clock.
